# 🔍 Drain Detective

> **"Other apps tell you which app used your battery. Drain Detective tells you what situation your phone was in."**
> 
> *Track how fast the battery drops, show which hour drained the most, and uncover the physical context behind every drop.*

Drain Detective is a native Android application built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Room SQLite, WorkManager, Foreground Services, and MVVM architecture (minSdk 26). Designed as a forensic battery investigator, it goes beyond generic app lists to reconstruct your phone's real-world environment during every battery loss event.

---

## 🚀 Flagship Features

### 1. 🔬 Context Fingerprint (Where was the phone when it drained?)
- **Multi-Sensor Environmental Sampling**: Low-power `ContextSensor` samples hardware sensors for ~4 seconds on each reading, then immediately unregisters all listeners to eliminate sensor drain:
  - **Proximity** (`Sensor.TYPE_PROXIMITY`): Identifies if the display is obstructed (e.g. inside a pocket or bag).
  - **Ambient Light** (`Sensor.TYPE_LIGHT`): Measures illuminance in lux ($< 5\text{ lux}$ indicates dark enclosure).
  - **Accelerometer Variance** (`Sensor.TYPE_ACCELEROMETER`): Measures 3-axis motion variance over the sampling window ($< 0.05\text{ m/s}^2$ indicates motionless on a desk; $\ge 0.35\text{ m/s}^2$ indicates walking or vehicle transit).
- **Pure Kotlin `ContextClassifier`**: Deterministically maps sensor values + charging/screen states to 6 situational contexts:
  - `ON_DESK` (🪑): Stationary flat surface, screen off, proximity clear.
  - `IN_POCKET_OR_BAG` (👖): Proximity covered, dark ambient light ($< 5\text{ lux}$).
  - `MOVING` (🚶): Active physical motion variance ($\ge 0.35$).
  - `CHARGING` (⚡): Connected to AC, USB, or wireless power.
  - `IN_USE` (📱): Display active and interactive.
  - `UNKNOWN` (❓): Default state when sensors are restricted in background.
- **Context Drain Bar Chart**: Visualizes consumption rates by situation (*"Pocket: 7.2%/hr, Desk: 2.1%/hr, In use: 11.8%/hr, Moving: 6.4%/hr"*).
- **"Pocket Cooking" Overheat Warning**: If the device is inside a pocket/bag, temperature rises $\ge 37.5^\circ\text{C}$, and drain is acute, posts an instant high-priority alert with cooldown (supports Android 13+ `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`).
- **"Detective on Duty" Foreground Service**: Android strictly limits background sensor access; Drain Detective provides an opt-in foreground service with a persistent notification for continuous 15-minute context sampling.

---

### 2. 👻 Ghost Race (Today vs Your Personal Best Day)
- **Automatic Ghost Benchmark**: `GhostRaceAnalyzer` scans historical days and identifies your personal **Best Day** (the day with the lowest average discharge rate).
- **24-Hour Diurnal Overlay**: Renders today's live battery curve (solid white line with glowing tracker) over the ghost curve (faint, dashed teal line) on a custom Compose Canvas aligned by time of day ($00:00 \to 24:00$).
- **Ahead / Behind Forensic Banner**:
  - *"You're +6% ahead of your ghost! 🏎️"* (Outperforming personal best).
  - *"You're -8% behind your ghost 🐢"* (Quantifies difference and extracts the primary culprit from context data: high screen time, pocket thermal trapping, or mobile transit handovers).
- **Graceful Calibrating State**: When fewer than 2 days are recorded, explains how the benchmark calibrates.

---

### 3. 📦 Black Box (10-Minute Flight Recorder & Scrubber)
- **Acute Spike Detection**: Triggered automatically when an acute battery drop rate spike is detected ($\ge 3\%$ drop in a single 15-minute interval or $\ge 10\%/\text{hr}$).
- **High-Resolution Flight Recording**: Captures high-frequency snapshots every 20 seconds for 10 minutes (30 data points) capturing level, temperature, context, screen, and radio state.
- **Room Persistence**: Stored as an `Incident` entity in Room SQLite with full telemetry JSON.
- **Interactive Replay Scrubber**: An interactive slider on the Incidents screen allows judges and users to scrub through all 30 points step-by-step and inspect the flight recorder verdict.

---

### 4. 📁 Case File & Suspect Lineup
- **Forensic Case Dialog**: Tapping the worst hour hero card or any bar/heatmap cell opens an in-depth Case File displaying:
  - Drain rate vs your baseline (*"-14% drop — 3.2x your average"*).
  - Environmental matrix: Screen minutes, battery temperature, cellular vs WiFi split, and physical context.
  - Plain-language verdict generated by rule-based reasoning.
- **Suspect Lineup (`UsageStatsManager`)**: Top 3 foreground applications active during that hour with duration and resolved icons. If `PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS` is not granted, displays a friendly explainer card and **never crashes**.

---

### 5. ⚡ Judge Demonstration Tools
- **Instant 3-Day Seeder**: Located in the Settings tab, the *"Seed Full Demo Data"* button populates:
  1. 72 hours (288 snapshots) covering all 6 contexts (`ON_DESK`, `IN_POCKET_OR_BAG`, `MOVING`, `CHARGING`, `IN_USE`).
  2. A dramatic **Pocket Cooking** anomaly at 6–7 PM (Hour 18, -14% drop, 39.5°C, mobile 5G).
  3. A clear **Ghost Day** (Day 2 with super-efficient 1.2%/hr baseline).
  4. A pre-recorded **Black Box Incident** with 30 scrubbable steps for instant replay.

## 🔒 Honest Transparency: Android Background Sensor Restrictions

Android intentionally restricts background apps from reading hardware sensors (proximity, light, continuous accelerometer) to protect privacy and prevent covert tracking. Drain Detective handles this honestly and safely:
1. **Foreground & Screen-On**: Sensors are sampled for ~4 seconds on each foreground reading.
2. **Detective on Duty (Opt-in Foreground Service)**: Runs a low-power foreground service with a persistent notification, granting sanctioned OS permission to sample sensors continuously every 15 minutes.
3. **Standard Background Worker (Fallback)**: When neither the app nor Detective on Duty is active, WorkManager saves battery level, temperature, and network, but sets `contextLabel = UNKNOWN` instead of fabricating false data.
